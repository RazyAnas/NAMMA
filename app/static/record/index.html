How to film for a good 3D model
  1. Lights on. Tilt the phone a little down so the floor is in view.
  2. Walk slowly along the walls, facing into the room. Don't spin on the spot.
  3. Go round twice: once at chest height, once lower or higher.
  4. Finish where you started.
  5. Plain walls, glass and mirrors are hard: include a textured object in frame.
  6. Optional: put a tape measure or a measured door in view to check real size.

Camera only mode records at 60 fps where the phone allows and locks exposure and white balance after a moment. Hold still for 2 seconds when you press it.
