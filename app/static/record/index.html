How to film for a good 3D model
  1. Lights on. Hold the phone at chest height, tilted a little down so the floor is in view.
  2. Walk slowly along the walls, facing into the room. Stop briefly before turning; don't spin on the spot.
  3. Each room: one lap along the walls, a second lap the other way, then walk both diagonals.
  4. Film one room at a time, starting in the doorway, and finish where you started.
  5. Plain walls, glass and mirrors are hard: include a textured object in frame.
  6. Optional: put a tape measure or a measured door in view to check real size.

Exposure, white balance and focus lock after a moment, so the same wall keeps the same brightness in every frame. Hold still for 2 seconds after you press Record, pointing at the brightest part of the room. Turn HDR off in your camera settings if the phone has a switch for it.
