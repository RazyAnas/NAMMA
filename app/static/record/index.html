How to film for a good 3D model
  1. Settings first: video HDR, scene optimiser and "super steady" stabilisation off. Lights on, blinds open.
  2. Hold the phone upright at chest height, aimed at eye level. Use the widest lens.
  3. Walk slowly, about one step a second. To change direction: stop, turn slowly, walk on. Never spin on the spot.
  4. Each room: start under the door frame looking in, walk one lap close to the walls, a second lap the other way, then cross both diagonals corner to corner. Then get close (30–60 cm) to anything you want sharp.
  5. Cross doorways through the middle, looking ahead. Don't stop the video between rooms.
  6. Plain walls, glass and mirrors are hard: tape up a poster or patterned item while filming, remove it after.

The recorder films in 4K at 30 fps where the phone allows (otherwise 1080p at 60 fps). Before it starts you see a preview with automatic exposure: adjust brightness, ISO, shutter and colour if needed, tap "Lock exposure and colour" when it looks natural, then Start.
